# CJ, in particles — personality concept

The engine stays: the WebGL particle stage, the one-function-of-scroll story,
the acted-out project scenes. What changes is the story it tells, the look, and
the voice. The current story (knot → schema → flow → surface) is any
developer's process. This one is CJ's life — something nobody else can copy.

Goal: a visitor thinks _"if he can build this, what can't he do?"_ — and then
remembers the jollof.

---

## Voice

- English, Lagos in the details. No Pidgin.
- Funny through the storytelling, not through labels. Never call CJ "annoying".
- Short, concrete, a little cheeky. Lines a friend would read out loud.
- No photos, no voice. Personality comes from story, motion and copy.

## Look

The current dark ink + single orange accent + serif italic is the default
award-site look right now. Proposed instead — warm, light, printed:

| Token     | Value     | Role                           |
| --------- | --------- | ------------------------------ |
| paper     | `#F3EADB` | background (rice-cream)        |
| ink       | `#1A120D` | type, most particles           |
| jollof    | `#B8321A` | accent                         |
| plantain  | `#E9A93B` | second accent, sparingly       |

- Paper grain over everything; particles read as ink and pigment on paper.
- A bold, slightly odd display face (not Inter / Geist / the usual serif italic).
